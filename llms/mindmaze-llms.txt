# MindMaze Therapeutics

> MindMaze Therapeutics Holding SA (SIX: MMTX) is a Swiss precision-neurotherapeutics company headquartered in Lausanne, founded in 2012 by Tej Tadi as an EPFL spin-off and formed in its current shape in December 2025 when successor entity NeuroX completed a business combination with Relief Therapeutics. It builds digital neurotherapeutics - gamified therapy software paired with proprietary sensors and AI-driven analytics - for stroke, Parkinson's disease and at-risk aging, delivered across the continuum of care from hospital to outpatient to home, and deployed in more than 250 clinics and rehabilitation centers globally.

MindMaze is a regulated medical-device and digital-therapeutics vendor, not a software platform business. As of 2026-08-01 it publishes **no developer portal, no API documentation, no SDKs, no CLI, no sandbox and no product API contract of any kind**. `api.mindmaze.com`, `developer.mindmaze.com`, `docs.mindmaze.com` and every other developer-shaped hostname fail to resolve — do not assume or invent one. There is no MCP server, no GraphQL surface, no A2A agent card and no GitHub organization.

The one machine-readable surface that does exist is the **WordPress core REST API** behind the corporate site, served anonymously at `https://mindmazetherapeutics.com/wp-json/`. It is a real, callable, read-only content API over 450 posts, 49 pages, 1,070 media items and 11 categories. Because MindMaze is a SIX-listed issuer, that post corpus is dominated by regulatory disclosure: 309 EQS posts, 162 EQS ad-hoc announcements, 147 other investor-relations items and 27 press releases. It is a corporate content API — it carries no clinical, device or patient data.

**Content signals.** `mindmazetherapeutics.com/robots.txt` publishes a Cloudflare-managed Content Signals policy of `search=yes, ai-train=no, use=reference`, asserts an Article 4 EU DSM reservation of rights, and explicitly disallows GPTBot, ClaudeBot, CCBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, Amazonbot, Bytespider and meta-externalagent. Treat retrieved content as reference-only and do not use it for model training.

## The one machine-readable surface

- [WordPress REST API index](https://mindmazetherapeutics.com/wp-json/): 18 namespaces, 466 registered routes, self-describing
- `GET /wp-json/wp/v2/posts?categories=24` — EQS ad-hoc regulatory announcements
- `GET /wp-json/wp/v2/posts?categories=8` — press releases
- `GET /wp-json/wp/v2/pages` — product, platform and research pages
- `GET /wp-json/wp/v2/media?mime_type=application/pdf` — investor presentations and report attachments
- `GET /wp-json/wp/v2/search?search=<term>` — cross-content search
- Anonymous, read-only. `per_page` caps at 100. Paginate with the `Link: rel="next"` header and read `X-WP-Total`. Errors are `{code, message, data:{status}}`, not RFC 9457. Do not call `/wp/v2/users`.
